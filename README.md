#  UpadhiSathi AI
### AI-Powered Rural Employment Information Assistant

UpadhiSathi AI is a proposed mobile-friendly application designed to help rural employment workers understand their work details, attendance and wage payment information easily.

##  Live Prototype

https://upadhisathi.netlify.app

##  Problem Statement

Rural employment workers often face difficulties in knowing:
- Where and when work is scheduled.
- Daily work timings.
- Whether their attendance is recorded.
- Whether wages are credited to their bank accounts.
- How much money was credited and when.
- How to report their problems.

##  Our Solution

We propose an AI-powered application that provides workers with easy access to their employment information through a simple interface and voice assistant.

##  Key Features

1. Work location and schedule details.
2. Daily work timings.
3. Attendance status tracking.
4. Expected wage information.
5. Wage payment status.
6. Credited amount and payment date.
7. Telugu voice assistant.
8. Voice-based complaint support.

##  Voice Assistant

Workers can ask questions in their own language, such as:
- Where is my work today?
- Is my attendance recorded?
- Has my wage been credited?
- How can I report a problem?

##  Target Users

Rural employment workers under MGNREGA.

##  Technologies

Technologies and integrations will be finalized during development.

##  Project Status

Working prototype with sample data. Real government data integration is planned for future development.

##  Future Scope
